What is data remediation and how does Lightup help simplify data remediation processes through automation? Data remediation is the systematic process of identifying and rectifying Data Quality issues that arise within a dataset. Lightup helps automate data remediation processes by offering an easy way for enterprise data teams to identify, analyze, and fix data issues at scale.
