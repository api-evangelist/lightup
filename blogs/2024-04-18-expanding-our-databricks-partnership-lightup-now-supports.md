We’re excited to announce that our partnership with Databricks has expanded with full support for Unity Catalog through Databricks Partner Connect. While Unity Catalog helps simplify data discovery in Databricks, that requires ongoing certification from a Data Quality Monitoring tool. Without that, Databricks users won’t know if they can trust the data they pull from Unity Catalog.
