By design, metrics aren’t automatically enabled by default in Lightup. Instead, metrics must be turned on or enabled, offering full control of metric configurations while mitigating unexpected query computation costs. With advancements in AI and Data Quality automation, we’re excited to announce that Lightup now supports plug-and-play Auto Metrics for metadata with our latest new feature: Zero-Config.
