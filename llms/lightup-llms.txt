# Lightup Data User docs Documentation

> Lightup is a next-generation data quality and data observability platform that empowers your team to quickly perform and repeat comprehensive data quality checks with ease. Automated workflows enable you to achieve complete data quality coverage of all your data assets, across your cloud and hybrid environments.
